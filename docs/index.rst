===============================================

Common account settings include:

* Profile information
* Password management
* Recovery options
* Security settings
* Display preferences
* Signature settings
* Spam management
* Email forwarding
* Auto-reply configuration
* Notification preferences

Reviewing these settings periodically helps keep your account organized and secure.

================

Common profile options include:

* Display name
* Contact information

* Language preference
* Time zone
* Regional settings

Keeping your profile updated helps recipients identify your emails correctly.

Password Settings
=================

Your password protects your account from unauthorized access.

You should update your password if:

* You suspect unauthorized access.
* Your password is weak.
* You recently experienced a phishing attempt.
* Your password has been exposed in a data breach.
* You have not changed it in several months.

A strong password should contain:

* Uppercase letters
* Lowercase letters
* Numbers
* Special characters
* At least 12 characters

Avoid using personal information or common words.

Recovery Settings
=================

Recovery settings help you regain access if you forget your password.

Important recovery options include:

* Recovery email address
* Recovery phone number
* Security verification methods

Keep this information updated to simplify future account recovery.

Email Forwarding Settings
=========================

This feature may be useful if:

* You manage multiple email accounts.
* You are changing email providers.
* You monitor business and personal accounts together.
* You want backup delivery of important emails.

Always verify forwarding rules to ensure they remain accurate.

Troubleshooting Account Settings Problems
=========================================

If your settings are not working as expected, try the following solutions.

Refresh the Browser
-------------------

Sometimes settings changes require a page refresh.

Clear Browser Cache
-------------------

Cached files may prevent updated settings from appearing correctly.

Clear:

* Browser cache
* Cookies
* Saved sessions

Restart the browser afterward.

Try Another Browser
-------------------

If one browser causes problems, try another supported browser.

Check Internet Connection
-------------------------

A weak connection may interrupt the saving process.

Ensure your internet connection is stable before making account changes.

Sign Out and Sign Back In
-------------------------

Logging out and signing back in often refreshes account settings.

Frequently Asked Questions
==========================

**How often should I review my account settings?**

Review your settings every few months or after any significant security event.

**Can I customize my email signature?**

Yes. Most email platforms allow you to create or modify your signature within the settings menu.

**Why are my settings not saving?**

Possible reasons include:

* Browser cache
* Internet interruption
* Temporary session timeout
* Browser compatibility issues

Refreshing the page or signing in again often resolves the issue.

**Will changing my settings delete my emails?**

No. Updating account settings does not remove your existing emails, contacts, folders, or attachments.

**Should I update my recovery information?**

Yes. Keeping your recovery email address and phone number current makes it much easier to recover your account if you ever forget your password.
